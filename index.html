Week 2 ⏱️ 50 Minutes 👥 Group & Peer Exercise

Research Challenge: Designing for Beginner Fitness Customers

📍 Scenario & Mission

You are designing a booking and membership website for a local fitness studio catering specifically to beginners.

⚠️ Your job is NOT to design the website yet. Your job is to answer: What do beginner customers actually need from the service and the website?

⏱️ 50-Minute Workshop Breakdown

1
00:00 - 05:00 — Understand the Scenario

Who are the users? What makes a beginner different from an experienced gym user? Identify assumptions to test.

2
05:00 - 10:00 — Research Questions

Formulate 3–5 core research questions as a class to guide your investigation.

3
10:00 - 25:00 — Secondary Research

Search credible sources (NHS, government, professional orgs, existing studio sites) for evidence.

4
25:00 - 35:00 — Findings to Insights

Synthesize raw data: Move from Information $\rightarrow$ Finding $\rightarrow$ Insight $\rightarrow$ Design Implication.

5
35:00 - 45:00 — Classmate Research

Draft 3 primary interview questions based on your secondary findings and poll your peers.

6
45:00 - 50:00 — Rapid Share & Debrief

Share one key finding: "We found that..." $\rightarrow$ "This could mean that..."

📝 Student Activity Worksheet

Fill out the following sections during the 50-minute workshop sprint.

Part 1: What Do We Need to Know?

Write 3 to 5 research questions that define what you need to find out about beginner users.

Part 2: Secondary Research Sprint

Find useful information from existing, credible sources (NHS, government, fitness industry, academic, studio sites).

🔍 Source Credibility Checklist:
  • Who produced the information? Is the source trustworthy?
  • Is it recent enough and relevant to beginner fitness customers?
  • Does it directly help answer your research questions?
Research Question Source (Name / URL) Key Finding Why is it useful?

Part 3: From Finding to Insight

Choose your strongest finding from Part 2 and translate it into a design recommendation.

💡 Worked Example:
  • What did we find? Beginners are uncertain about what to expect when attending a new fitness class.
  • What does this tell us about users? New customers need reassurance and clear information before booking.
  • What might this mean for the website? The website should clearly explain the class difficulty level, instructor details, what happens during the session, and what customers should bring.

Based on your secondary research, write 3 quick questions to ask your peers to test your assumptions.
