Week 1 ⏱️ 47 Mins 👥 Group Exercise

Activity: UX Detective

🎯 Goal: Identify and analyse frustrating digital experiences using User-Centred Design (UCD) thinking. The aim is to understand the problem rather than design a solution.

⏱️ Workshop Breakdown

1
00:00 - 05:00 — Individual Reflection

Think of a website or app that frustrated you recently (e.g., hard-to-find contact page, confusing checkout, unclear navigation). Write it on a sticky note.

2
05:00 - 15:00 — Share & Select

In groups of 4, share examples and pick one problem statement. Avoid suggesting a solution at this stage!

3
15:00 - 27:00 — Be a UX Detective

Investigate: WHO is experiencing it? WHAT are they trying to do? WHERE does it occur? WHY is it hard? IMPACT?

4
27:00 - 37:00 — What Would You Need to Know?

Identify 3 questions to investigate if this is a real problem for others. Put yourself in the user's shoes.

5
37:00 - 47:00 — Quick Share

Present: Our problem, user, goal, what we don't know, and how we could investigate it.

Week 2 ⏱️ 50 Mins 👥 Group & Peer Exercise

Activity: Research Practice (Beginner Fitness Studio)

🎯 Goal: Discover user needs, concerns, and expectations before jumping into code or design using secondary research and classmate polling.

📍 Scenario

You are designing a booking and membership website for a local fitness studio catering specifically to beginners. Answer: What do beginner customers actually need from the service and the website?

📝 Secondary Research & Insight Worksheet

1. What did we find? 2. What does this tell us about users? 3. Design Implication for Website
Week 3 ⏱️ 50 Mins 💻 Secondary Research & Audit

Activity: Competitor Benchmarking & UI Auditing

🎯 Goal: Analyze existing beginner-focused fitness studio websites to identify design patterns, strengths, and user experience gaps.

📍 Scenario & Objective

Before creating a new design, UX researchers conduct a Competitor Audit. Examine 2 existing local or national fitness studio websites to see how they onboard beginners.
